The user downloaded the original FBX archive from the above model page. The source FBX and supplied textures are preserved in `source` and `textures`.

Project modifications: rebuilt URP materials, converted roughness/opacity textures, adjusted paint and glass, closed the driver's door, separated merged wheel and steering geometry for animation, and added vehicle physics and two camera views. Derived assets are stored separately in `Derived` and `Materials`.
