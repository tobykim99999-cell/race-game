# Environment Materials

Source: Poly Haven public asset library. License: CC0 1.0.

- Asphalt 02: https://polyhaven.com/a/asphalt_02
  - 2K diffuse, OpenGL normal, roughness, AO and displacement maps downloaded through the public Poly Haven API.
- Aerial Grass Rock: https://polyhaven.com/a/aerial_grass_rock
  - 2K diffuse, OpenGL normal, roughness, AO and displacement maps downloaded through the public Poly Haven API.
- Forest Ground 04: https://polyhaven.com/a/forest_ground_04
  - 2K roughness, AO, displacement and OpenGL normal maps added for the forest floor and gravel shoulders.
- Sand 01: https://polyhaven.com/a/sand_01
  - 2K roughness, AO, displacement and OpenGL normal maps added for the desert ground.
- Snow 01: https://polyhaven.com/a/snow_01
  - 2K roughness, AO, displacement and OpenGL normal maps added for alpine snow.

License: https://polyhaven.com/license

The downloaded files were verified against the MD5 values in the provider's file manifest. The landscape geometry and circuit meshes are generated locally. These textures have been assigned to Unity URP materials and a Unity Terrain layer.
