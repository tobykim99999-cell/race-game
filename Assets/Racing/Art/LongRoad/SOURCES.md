The generated terrain, road, markings and game-effect geometry remain local; scanned fir instances and boulders provide the visible environment models. Asphalt, meadow, forest-ground, sand and snow PBR channels are credited separately in `../PolyHaven/SOURCES.md`.

Design reference: the varied biomes, long rally routes and night racing described at https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2023/02/23/forza-horizon-5-rally-adventure-available-march-29/ . No Forza assets or track geometry are included.
