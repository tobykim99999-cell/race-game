# Vehicle Audio

## Engine recording

Title: racing car engine sound loops
Author: domasx2
Source: https://opengameart.org/content/racing-car-engine-sound-loops
Downloaded file: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/loop_0.wav
License: CC0 1.0, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The author states that the current files were remade from a public-domain recording. `engine-source.wav` preserves the downloaded recording. `engine-loop.wav` removes DC offset, normalizes volume and crossfades the loop boundary. Runtime pitch and filtering are adjusted by the game. This is a generic engine recording, not a recording of this specific Porsche model.

## Supporting effects

`road-loop.wav`, `tyre-loop.wav` and `collision.wav` are deterministic synthesized effects generated for this project by `Tools/generate_vehicle_audio.py`. They do not use third-party recordings.
